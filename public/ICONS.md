# Icon sources

New FastAPI, PowerShell, Windows, GitHub Actions, Vercel, Vitest, pytest, Three.js, Framer Motion, and ESLint SVGs come from [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon), under the included MIT license. Vercel, Three.js, and Framer Motion fills are white for the dark background.

The Pydantic SVG comes from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), under the included CC0 license, with Pydantic's brand color applied.

Brand names and logos belong to their respective owners. Existing icon assets are retained.
