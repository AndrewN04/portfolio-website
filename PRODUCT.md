# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective employers, collaborators, and peers who want to understand Andrew Nguyen's software work and get in touch.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio presents Andrew Nguyen, a Texas-based full-stack developer, his skills, selected projects, and contact options. Success means visitors can quickly understand his work and navigate to a project or contact channel.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- A single-page portfolio with About, Skills, Projects, and Contact sections.
- Projects link to their existing public repositories; A04 Agent is identified as planned and has no public project link.
- Contact form posts to its existing Formspree endpoint.
- Preserve the terminal-inspired brand, Space Grotesk and Fira Code, automatic role typewriter and continuously moving RGB gradient, five-project carousel pages with keyboard/pagination controls, footer hover spotlight, and responsive accessible navigation.
- The typewriter and RGB gradient intentionally continue under reduced-motion settings.

## Brand Commitments

The site is a04.dev for Andrew Nguyen. Retain its dark terminal and code-editor inspiration, syntax blue and green, Space Grotesk, and Fira Code.

## Evidence on Hand

Project data and current status live in `app/lib/projects.ts`; skills live in `app/lib/skills.ts`. Existing portfolio copy, repository links, CV PDF, GitHub and LinkedIn links, and contact endpoint are in the repository. Do not invent project claims or imply planned work is complete.
